Indoor sensor

No BME280 / BMP280 / BME680 answers at 0x76 or 0x77. The clock looks again every 30 seconds, so plugging it in does not need a reboot. Check: the 4-pin connector on the left edge of the board (marked 3V3 GND IO1 IO2) is the I2C port; SDA goes to IO1 and SCL to IO2; a Qwiic-style cable has GND and 3V3 in the opposite order to this board, so wire by the labels, not the colours; the sensor's own pins are usually VIN/3V3, GND, SCL, SDA.

Last 3 hours, one point per minute. Arrows compare with the reading one hour ago (temperature, humidity, air) and three hours ago (pressure, scaled while the history is shorter).

Tides (NOAA CO-OPS)

High and low water predictions for one NOAA tide station (US coasts, Great Lakes excluded), datum MLLW. "Find nearest stations" downloads NOAA's station list in your browser and lists the six closest to the coordinates above; pick one and Save.

Indoor sensor (BME280 / BMP280 / BME680 on the I2C header)

Wire 3.3 V, GND, SDA (GPIO 1) and SCL (GPIO 2). Keep the sensor a few inches from the panel and the controller, or set a temperature offset. Trend arrows: up / down when the change over the window exceeds 0.5 °C, 3 % or 1 hPa; a double arrow means three times that. The air-quality score is relative: 75 % from the gas resistance against a clean-air baseline the clock learns (kept across reboots), 25 % from how close humidity is to 40 %. It needs about five minutes after power-up and improves over the first day.

Pages in the rotation

Tick a page to put it in the rotation, then drag it by the handle, or use the arrows, to set the order. A ticked page still hides itself when it has nothing to show, so the sea-temperature page stays out of the way if your tide station has no thermometer.

Sleep timer

minutes

Runs normally for that long, then fades the panel out and holds it dark until you cancel it or the clock reboots. Handy at bedtime without touching the night-mode schedule.

Your own dates

Up to four dates of your own, on top of the nine built-in holidays. On that day the clock and date take the colour you pick. Yours win if a built-in holiday falls on the same date.

Colours

Weather radar

Radar from NOAA's nowCOAST map service: the MRMS reflectivity mosaic with birds, insects and clutter filtered out, the same product weather apps use. The unfiltered Iowa Mesonet NEXRAD composite stays available as an alternative. Either is cropped by the map server to the panel around your location; the newest image plus the 5 to 50 minutes-ago frames make an eleven-frame loop. A blinking cross marks home. The base map (coastline, land and water from NASA GIBS) is fetched once for your location and radius. US coverage only for the radar itself.

Stopwatch

Countdowns

Up to three dates. The "event" page shows whichever is nearest, counting the days. Tick "every year" for a birthday or anniversary; leave it clear and give a full year for a one-off.

HUB75 panel (changes need a reboot)

Test pattern: solid red, green, blue, white → border with coloured corner markers and a diagonal → colour gradients and a grey ramp → info text. Stray pixels in a corner during the solid or gradient phases point at latch timing: raise latch blanking to 3 or 4 (applies without reboot), then try clock phase off.

Symptoms: dark panel → try FM6126A or ICN2038S; washed-out colours → FM6124; ghost columns → latch blanking 3-4 and clock phase off; flicker → higher min refresh or 6-bit depth.

Alert chime

The EAS and NOAA tones are simulations of the broadcast signals for your own clock; this is not an official alerting device. Long sounds (sirens, EAS) play once per trigger and are never queued up.

Hourly chime

Quiet hours silence the hourly chime, so it will not wake you. It also stays quiet while an alarm is ringing or snoozed.

Spoken announcements

The clock speaks with a pre-rendered voice pack (about 4 MB, Piper TTS with the public-domain LJ Speech voice) that it downloads once from the update site and keeps in its flash; new packs arrive with updates. Announcements follow the chime's quiet hours and use the same volume.

Pushbullet

The clock registers itself as a device called "Matrix Weather Clock" in your Pushbullet account, so you can push text to it from the phone app (choose the device) or from IFTTT / scripts. Leave the token as *** to keep the stored one.

Webhook

Posts a small JSON object to any URL you choose, so it works with ntfy, Gotify, Home Assistant, Discord or your own script without an account. The two field names let you match what the service expects: ntfy wants title and message, which is the default. Leave the header value as *** to keep the stored one.

Infrared remote

Wire a VS1838B / TSOP38238 receiver module: OUT to the GPIO above, VCC to 3V3, GND to GND. Any NEC remote works directly (the $1 21-key remotes, most TV and set-top box remotes); other protocols are learned by their timing. Press Learn on a row, then the button on the remote, and the code fills in. Save when done.

Phone remote

Open /remote on a phone or tablet and add it to the home screen for a full-screen remote with big buttons: dismiss, next page, radar, forecast, hourly graph, timers, snooze and stop, brightness, night mode, mute, demo, refresh. Anything else can use POST /api/action?name=next_page; GET /api/actions lists the names.

Network

Leave a password as *** to keep the stored one. After saving new credentials the clock connects; the setup AP disappears once it is online. With two networks saved the clock alternates between them while it retries. A static address takes effect on the next connection, so save and let it reconnect.

Danger zone

Factory reset erases every setting stored on the clock: WiFi credentials, location and time zone, alert, display, panel and audio settings, alarms, and the Pushbullet token. The clock then reboots into the setup access point (MatrixWeatherClock-XXXX / http://4.3.2.1/) as on first use. The firmware itself is not changed. Consider downloading a backup on the Update tab first.

Automatic updates

The clock reads the web installer's manifest, downloads the OTA image over HTTPS, checks its MD5 and reboots into the new version. Automatic installs wait while an alarm or timer is running.

Settings backup

Download backup

The backup contains WiFi passwords. Restoring applies every section and reboots if panel settings changed.
